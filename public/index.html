how to play
  1. Every round, everyone adds a trap to the page. Traps stay round after round, so the page gets harder and harder.
  2. Take turns putting: drag back from anywhere and let go. You get 4 strokes.
  3. Sink it for 3 points + 1 per stroke left, and +2 if you did it in the fewest shots. But if everyone sinks it, the sink points don't count!
  4. Your trap stops someone? +1 for you. Bombs blow up anything placed nearby.
  5. Nobody makes it two rounds in a row? The page is impossible, so it's torn out for a fresh one.
