Pen Golf

New game

How many players? (pass the mouse around)

how to play
  1. Before each hole, everyone picks a trap and places it on the page.
  2. Take turns putting: drag back from anywhere and let go. You get 4 strokes.
  3. Sink it for 3 points + 1 per stroke left. But if everyone sinks it, nobody scores!
  4. Your trap stops someone? +1 for you. First to 15 wins.
